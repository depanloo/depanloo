## 🚀 About Me 
I'm a Data Science graduate from Institut Teknologi Sumatera, highly passionate about transforming complex, messy datasets into clear stories and actionable insights. 

My focus as a Data Analyst is on building interactive dashboards, validating statistical datasets, and utilizing predictive modeling to help organizations make data-driven decisions. During my internship at the Central Bureau of Statistics (BPS), I contributed directly to the 2023 Agricultural Census by processing data and developing demographic dashboards using Google Looker Studio. 

When I'm not writing SQL queries or building visual reports, I enjoy organizing events and managing teams, having previously led student development programs focused on sports and arts at my university. I love the "aha!" moment when raw data reveals something new and useful.
